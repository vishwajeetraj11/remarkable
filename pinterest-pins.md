## Publishing checklist

1. Claim `remarkable.vishwajeet.co` in Pinterest Business settings so Pins receive attribution and domain analytics.
2. Create a board named **Free reMarkable Templates & Printables**.
3. Publish one Pin at a time with the matching creative, title, description, and exact destination URL above.
4. Use each destination URL unchanged so Pinterest traffic is separated by creative in analytics.
5. Check the destination on mobile after publishing and confirm it opens the generator, not the homepage.
