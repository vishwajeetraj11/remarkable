## Design Context

### Users

Remarkable Skills serves owners of all e-ink tablets, including reMarkable,
Supernote, BOOX, and Kindle Scribe devices, as well as people who occasionally
print the generated PDFs. They come to the site to create a useful, correctly
sized worksheet, planner, puzzle, or template and move it onto their device
without creating an account.

### Brand Personality

Practical, calm, and trustworthy. Copy should explain what a tool does, who it
helps, and how to use the result without hype or search-oriented filler.

### Aesthetic Direction

Quiet, paper-led utility. The interface should feel like a well-organized
stationery drawer: neutral, legible, and precise, with the generated page as the
visual focus. Support light and dark application themes while keeping document
previews faithful to white paper and e-ink output.

### Design Principles

1. Show the actual result before asking for a download.
2. Offer only configuration that meaningfully changes the document.
3. Explain the workflow with concrete, page-specific guidance.
4. Make defaults useful, and provide a browser-generated one-page sample.
5. Prefer evidence and plain language over marketing claims or SEO padding.
