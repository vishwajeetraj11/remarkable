## Positioning that is accurate and trustworthy

Use this wording consistently:

> I built Remarkable Skills, a free collection of reMarkable-ready templates and puzzle generators. PDFs are generated in your browser, and their contents are not uploaded. No account, watermark, or email address is required. The site uses product analytics, but it does not send the contents of generated PDFs to analytics providers.

Do **not** say “no tracking” or “completely private.” The site uses PostHog and Microsoft Clarity, plus a consent-gated Google Ads tag. “PDF contents stay in the browser” is the precise differentiator.

## A reputation-building plan

### Phase 1 — Become recognizable through help

For the first 7–10 days:

1. Comment on three to five current questions where you can add something concrete.
2. Make at least half of those comments completely link-free.
3. If you do link, use the exact relevant template page rather than the homepage.
4. Disclose “I built this” before the link.
5. Return to replies and fix misunderstandings quickly.
6. Do not mass-DM members or post a second launch announcement while the first is pending.

### Phase 2 — Build in public from requests

Pick one request with a clear owner, such as the sales visit log, writing planner, client index, or health tracker.

1. Ask three requirement questions in the original thread.
2. Build a small first version.
3. Invite the requester to test it on their actual device.
4. Publish what changed because of their feedback.
5. Credit the community member if they agree.

This creates a stronger trust loop than repeatedly saying the app is free.

### Phase 3 — Establish a useful cadence

Recommended cadence:

- **Weekly:** one thoughtful answer to a recurring workflow problem
- **Every 1–2 weeks:** one free template created from a named community request
- **Monthly:** a compact “what I learned from reMarkable users” post
- **Occasionally:** a free puzzle, because the observed word-search post earned 35 reactions and 16 comments

Avoid posting a link every day. The goal is to become “the person who listens and builds useful reMarkable PDFs,” not “the person promoting a template site.”

4. **Health Log** — configurable fields for sleep, CPAP, symptoms, medication, appointments and notes.
5. **Simplified Hyperlinked Planner** — monthly, weekly and daily pages without visual clutter.
6. **Privacy-conscious workflow guide** — accurately distinguish local PDF creation from later device/cloud transfer.
7. **Weekly puzzle format** — themed word searches or rotating puzzle types with answer keys.
8. **Template request voting** — let the community choose the next build and publish the result.

## Comment formula

Use this four-part structure:

1. Refer to the person's exact situation.
2. Give one useful answer before mentioning your app.
3. Disclose your relationship to the resource.
4. Ask one small feedback question.

Example:

> For repeated customer visits, I’d keep the same fields on every page so follow-ups don’t disappear inside free-form notes. I built a free Client Call Notes generator that may be a useful starting point: [direct link]. I’m the developer. Which field would make it work for your sales process—next action, opportunity value, or follow-up date?

- Facebook-referred sessions
- `template_generator_started`
- `pdf_generated`

- Facebook visit-to-PDF-generation rate
- Template and device breakdowns
- Returning Facebook visitors

https://remarkable.vishwajeet.co/templates/client-call?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=rm_group_help&utm_content=sales_visit_thread
